## Vein 3: Dupe / exploit / hacked-item economic history

Ranked candidates (details from the researcher; verify specific artifacts before carding):

1. **The "08 items"** (patch 1.08 uniques with impossible/superior stats). CONFIRMED phenomenon.
   1.08 (Jun 29 2001) shipped uniques with stronger/different mods than 1.09/1.10 later set;
   stats bake in at creation, so an 08 copy keeps its block forever. Prestige collectibles,
   duped forward from a tiny original pool. Specific bases: 08 Titan's Revenge, 08 helms.
   NOTE: we already have 1.08 Vampire Gaze + 1.08 Valkyrie Wing. An "08 items" card would be
   a META/explainer, or fold in as context. Watch redundancy.

2. **Mephisto Fused Uniques**: double-unique drops welded one item's stats onto another's base
   slot (e.g., Wizardspike-on-gauntlets, Undead-Crown-on-ring). DISPUTED / folklore-leaning.
   Card only as documented folklore, or if a screenshot is verified.

3. **Siggard's Stealth "Bug Belt"**: distinct from the legit 1.10 rename we already card. A
   hacked/fused belt (Nosferatu's + Arachnid's on one belt). DISPUTED / folklore.

4. **The 1.09 trade-window dupe wave**: CONFIRMED era; industrial-scale duping, SOJ/Windforce
   hit; birthed permed-vs-unpermed buyer anxiety. Event/phenomenon. Keep distinct from our
   SOJ-inflation card (focus on the trust/permed story, not prices).

5. **The 2003 Great Purge / ban wave**: CONFIRMED. ~36,000+ accounts closed, 28,000+ CD-keys
   disabled, bugged stock ~99% wiped around the 1.10 transition (Oct 28 2003); closed the
   open-Bnet->closed-realm transfer route. This is WHY survivors (08s, bugged items) are rare.
   Strong standalone event card; ties our whole contraband/legacy set together.

6. **The D2R 2.5 dupe wave (2022)**: CONFIRMED. Fresh-ladder economy destabilized in <3 weeks;
   Ber/Jah/Sur, Infinity, Enigma flooded; duped gear filmed poofing on camera. Modern mirror
   of 2003. Event/phenomenon; iconic visual = poofing Enigma.

7. **Open Bnet items smuggled onto closed realms**: CONFIRMED history. Editors (Jamella) made
   arbitrary items; a transfer route walked them onto ladder pre-~mid-2002, exposed early 2003.
   The "why impossible items exist at all" umbrella. Phenomenon.

8. **Bugged charm affixes**: pre-1.10 small charms with impossible affixes (Small Charm of
   Inertia = +5% FRW on a small charm; extreme-poison "Anthrax"-type ~451 psn/12s). DISPUTED.
   Small, verifiable "impossible legit-looking" curiosity. 1.10 changed the affix pool.

Watch-outs: Fused Uniques + hybrid Bug Belt are shakiest (folklore). Keep SOJ-inflation distinct
from the 1.09 dupe-wave card. Siggard's rename (legit) is NOT the hacked Bug Belt.

## Vein 5: Items/events that changed the game (mechanics born from items)

1. **Wirt's Leg / Cow King lockout**: CONFIRMED. Wirt's Leg + ToTP opens the Secret Cow Level.
   If the Cow King dies in a game YOU CREATED, that character can never reopen the cow portal on
   that difficulty again (per-character, per-difficulty, keyed to the game creator, no reset).
   Removed in D2R. NOTE: we cut Wirt's Leg on value; RECONSIDER purely for this hidden-rule story.
   Cardable item: Wirt's Leg. Strong "wait, THAT's a rule?" payload.

2. **Hidden item-ID "youngest copy survives" dupe scanner**: CONFIRMED system (phrasing is community
   description). Items carry a hidden unique ID; a periodic clash-scan + on-save scan deletes the
   older of two matching IDs. The older holder logs in to find it gone. Whole invisible anti-cheat
   layer exists because of duping. No cardable item; attach to a high-value dupe target.

3. **Ladder system (1.10, Oct 28 2003)**: CONFIRMED. Sealed, resetting economy built to escape the
   dupe-ruined one; best runewords gated ladder-only to pull players in. Cardable via a ladder-only
   runeword (e.g., Spirit / Insight) as the face of it.

4. **Zod Rune / ethereal-indestructible**: CONFIRMED. Zod = Indestructible, the only way to save an
   eth item and keep its bonus. 1.09 Zod-in-eth bug (bonus-durability eth ruined; no-bonus eth made
   indestructible), fixed 1.10. Cardable: Zod Rune. Partial overlap with our Glitched Arreat's. Frame
   the RUNE + mechanic, not the bug.

5. **Pandemonium Event / Hellfire Torch (1.11)**: CONFIRMED. Keys -> organs -> Uber Tristram -> Torch.
   Second Bnet-only event after Uber Diablo; early raid template. We already card the legacy Torch;
   this is the EVENT behind it (could enrich the Torch history rather than a new card).

6. **Currency evolution: SOJ -> High Runes -> d2jsp Forum Gold**: CONFIRMED phenomenon (exact dates
   fuzzy). Each currency died by duping; money fled to what dupers couldn't reach (ending off-game in
   FG). Cardable: a High Rune (Ber/Jah) as the post-SOJ "gold standard." Distinct from our SOJ card.

7. **Legacy bugged-item class (1.08-1.10 window)**: CONFIRMED overlaps Vein 3 #1/#5 (08 items, Zod-bug).

8. **Token of Absolution / respec history (1.13)**: CONFIRMED item added 1.13; original D2 had NO respec
   (permanent points), brutal after the 1.10 synergy overhaul. Token (4 Essences from act bosses) +
   Akara free respec ended build-anxiety. Cardable: Token of Absolution. Causal "why" is community
   inference, not a dev quote. Frame as "likely why."

9. **Enigma**: EXCLUDED (famous, meta not a rule).

Best-three per researcher: Wirt's Leg/Cow King lockout, the item-ID scanner, Hellfire Torch/Pandemonium.

## Vein 2: Patch-history legacy / pre-nerf items

Pattern: patch 1.09 (Aug 20 2001) "Altered stats of many Unique items"; 1.08 copies keep pre-nerf mods.
All top-5 CONFIRMED (before/after + patch). These fit our existing 08/legacy theme exactly.

1. **08 Windforce (Hydra Bow)**: CONFIRMED. 1.08: **+35 Dex** + Heal Stamina 30%. 1.09: cut to +5 Dex,
   +10 Str. Everything else same. Live collector SKU ("Windforce 08"). Strong legacy card.
2. **08 The Grandfather (Colossus Blade)**: CONFIRMED. 1.08: **+175 flat Life, +25% max Life, +25% max
   Mana** (survivability sword). 1.09 stripped those for per-level scaling. Fundamentally different item.
3. **08 Bul-Kathos' Wedding Band**: CONFIRMED. 1.08: **+20 flat Life, fixed 4% life leech**, +1 skills.
   1.09: life scales 0-49/level, leech variable 3-5%. Twink value (full mods at any level). (We rejected
   a *perfect modern* BK; the 08 legacy angle is different.)
4. **08 Highlord's Wrath (amulet)**: CONFIRMED before/after. 1.08: **30% FRW, no IAS, no Deadly Strike**.
   1.09: removed FRW, added +1 skills/20 IAS/level-scaling Deadly Strike. Collector oddity, instantly
   ID'd as pre-1.09. (We rejected modern Highlord's; 08 angle is different.)
5. **08 Herald of Zakarum (Gilded Shield)**: CONFIRMED diff. 1.08: **Cannot Be Frozen + 50% FBR**. 1.09
   reshuffled (30% FBR, no CBF, +2 pal/+2 combat). NOTE: we already card a roll-dependent modern HoZ.
   A legacy HoZ would be a 2nd HoZ; only add if we want it. Undead-bonus wording DISPUTED.
6. **Wizardspike (legacy Bone Knife)**: All Res +95 -> +75. Change attested, PATCH DISPUTED (the "40 in
   1.09" claim is a misread of a monster stat). Only card if patch verified.
7. **Blackoak Shield (pre-1.09)**: CONFIRMED via PRIMARY patch notes: 1.09 "fixed a bug where enhanced
   defense gained per level was improperly calculated (such as Blackoak Shield)"; same note names
   **Hellslayer** (per-level ED bug) and **Magewrath** (2h/missile dmg not applying). A "did you know"
   version-quirk curiosity, straight from the source.

Bonus (rune-flavored history card option): 1.09 renamed **Shae->Shael, Po->Io, Jo->Jah** and swapped
**Jah/Cham** weapon properties: primary patch notes. Parallels our Siggard's rename angle.

Avoid (reverse-legacy, old = weaker): Vipermagi, Oculus, Gull, Gavel of Pain (buffed later). String of
Ears (just fixed rolls, weak story). Shako 1.08 +40% max life = obscure but too famous a helm.

## Vein 1: Classic D2 (pre-LoD, 2000-2001) deep cuts

KEY GATE: exceptional & elite uniques did NOT exist in classic (LoD only). So classic endgame =
normal-tier uniques, classic sets, rares. Drop any "classic bow unique" (Witchwild/Goldstrike/
Skystrike are LoD). All below CONFIRMED classic unless noted.

1. **Ring generation order: Nagelring -> Manald Heal -> Stone of Jordan**: CONFIRMED mechanic. Unique
   rings roll in fixed priority; carry a Nagel + Manald and the next unique ring is forced to SoJ.
   Low-mlvl (~40) monsters ~1/31 SoJ vs mlvl84+ ~1/59, so low areas were better SoJ hunting. Card via
   **Manald Heal** or **Nagelring** ("the junk ring you carried to fish for SoJs"). SoJ itself already
   carded. Best pure "THAT mattered" mechanic. (Manald/Nagel exact stat lines need a fetch.)
2. **Bonesnap (Maul)**: CONFIRMED. Classic melee chase: Req Lvl 24, +200-300% ED, +40% Crushing Blow,
   +100% dmg undead, cold/fire res +30. Top melee weapon pre-LoD (no runewords/elites), Cube-upped.
   Cleanest classic chase item.
3. **Silks of the Victor (Ancient Armor)**: CONFIRMED. Req 28; +100-120% EDef, **+1 All Skills**, 5% ML.
   Best classic CASTER body armor because +skills barely existed pre-LoD. "Forgotten best-in-slot."
4. **Cow King must-not-die rule**: CONFIRMED (= Vein 5 #1). Classic locked you out permanently; D2R
   removed it. Card via Wirt's Leg or Cow King's Leathers.
5. **Iratha's Finery (set)**: CONFIRMED. 4 cheap pieces, all-res +20 + per-piece resists, HALF FREEZE
   (not CBF - that's Death's, carded). Classic resist-fix staple. Pieces: Coil(crown)/Collar(ammy)/
   Cord(belt)/Cuff(l.gauntlets).
6. **Goldskin (Full Plate Mail)**: CONFIRMED. Req 28; +120-150 EDef, **All Resistances +35**, 100% extra
   gold, ATD 10. Reads as a gold gimmick; the real classic prize was fixed +35 all-res on the chest.
7. **Heavenly Garb (Light Plate)**: CONFIRMED role (other classic caster armor: mana/MF/light radius),
   stat block PARTIAL: needs a fetch before carding.
8. **Classic level-99 grind**: CONFIRMED phenomenon. No Baal/Act5; 98->99 = ~291M XP (= all of 1->98).
   A classic 99 was a realm legend. No item.

Notes: Iratha's = Half Freeze, not CBF. Manald/Nagel + Heavenly Garb need stat fetches. Classic bow
endgame = rares (Carrion Song already covers it).

Researcher top-3: ring-gen order, Bonesnap, Silks of the Victor.
